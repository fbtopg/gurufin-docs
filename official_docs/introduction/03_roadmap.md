# Roadmap

This roadmap summarizes the planned development path for Gurufin Chain and the GX Stablecoin Network. Scope, sequencing, and release timing may change as testnet results, audits, partner readiness, and regulatory requirements evolve.

## Phase 1: Foundation (Completed)

* Core infrastructure for Gurufin Chain and the initial GX chains.
* Live Reserve Scanner (LRS) and automated mint/burn mechanisms.
* Guru-PEG implementation for fiat-indexed transaction fees.
* Independent security audit by Sherlock completed; publication of the full report is planned alongside mainnet launch materials.

## Phase 2: Expansion (In Progress)

* Public testnet launch.
* Guruswap Oracle Priced Reserve Swap (OPRS) deployment.
* GX stablecoin proof of concept with IBC connectivity.
* EVM Gateway and price oracle network integration.
* Ecosystem Development Fund setup and governance parameter implementation.

## Phase 3: Scale (Planned)

* Mainnet launch.
* Expansion of GX stablecoin support into additional jurisdictions.
* Multi-leg cross-currency PvP settlement and Layer-2 scaling.
* Advanced governance features, including research into quadratic voting.

## Phase 4: Global (Future)

* Regulated bridge integrations.
* Partnerships with regulated financial institutions and payment providers.
* Progressive transition toward broader community governance.
* Institutional-scale throughput across a multi-currency stablecoin network.

> Roadmap items describe intended direction, not a guarantee of launch timing or final feature scope.
